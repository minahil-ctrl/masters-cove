      How tiers work
      Likely
      The published requirements sit at or below your profile today.
      Target
      Holistic review, where your SOP, experience and letters can carry you over the line.
      Reach
      Averages above your profile, a hard test requirement or a very small intake.
      Unrated
      Not researched enough to judge yet.

      A healthy list usually mixes all three: a few reaches, mostly targets, and at least one or two likely options.
